# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Staff of Beverly Hills Academy, a private basic school in Ghana (Creche to JHS 3, about 917
students and 54 staff): proprietress, head, front office, bursar, class and subject teachers,
housemaster. Many are not tech-savvy. Teachers mostly use cheap Android phones with weak signal;
the office and bursar use desktops and laptops. Guardians use WhatsApp, not this app.

## Product Purpose

Replace paper and scattered Excel files for student records, fees, attendance, results and parent
messaging. Success: a teacher can do a daily task on first try without asking anyone.

## Operating Context

Three terms per academic year (Ghana Education Service). Internet and power drop often; offline
use arrives with sync in Sprint 4.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Mobile-first at 360 px, tap targets at least 44 px, plain English, `GHS 1,250.00`, `DD/MM/YYYY`,
Ghana phone formats. Every screen shows loading, empty, error, offline and not-allowed states.
Lists and documents print cleanly. Staff permissions are enforced by the API.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Beverly Hills Academy. Motto on the crest: "Seeking Wisdom & Integrity". Logos and
inspiration images: `docs/design/`. Light and dark modes.

## Evidence on Hand

School crest and emblem. Development data is generated; there is no real student data outside
production.

## Product Principles

- One screen, one job.
- Plain words a teacher already uses.
- Always show what happened.
- Fast and light before decorative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Labels on every input, sufficient contrast, keyboard navigable, reduced motion respected.
